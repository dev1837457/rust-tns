# Code of Conduct

Rust TNS aims to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants
are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- discuss code, evidence, and format behavior rather than people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience; and
- respect the licensing and privacy boundaries described in
  [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate
disruption, and knowingly submitting unsafe or improperly licensed material
are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
options. Do not post private reports, security details, proprietary files, or
personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports
fairly and may remove comments, close discussions, or decline contributions
that violate this code.
